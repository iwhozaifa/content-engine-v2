## 1. Identity

- **Creator name:** Aliyan Shah
- **Brand name:** Aliyan AI
- **Primary niche:** Practical AI, skills, income and leverage
- **Secondary niches:** Freelancing / remote work, small business systems
- **Market:** Pakistan first, then wider South Asia
- **Languages:** English dominant, Urdu / Roman Urdu secondary

## 2. Brand identity and positioning

- **Who I am:** I am an AI automation engineer who builds real systems and shows ambitious people how to turn AI skills into practical leverage and income.
- **What I want to be known for:** Turning AI into real income and leverage, not tool hype
- **Audience transformation:** Stuck → Skilled → Paid → Scaled
- **Differentiator:** I demonstrate working AI workflows from a builder’s perspective and combine practical skills with the psychology of execution — honest proof, calm big-brother guidance and no tool hype.
- **Never become:** an AI tool directory, a generic motivation page, a get-rich-quick brand, a freelancing-only page, a random opinion page

## 3. Target audience and niche

- **Primary audience:** Ambitious young South Asians (roughly 18–30), Pakistan-centred, into AI, high-value skills, freelancing/remote work, business and leverage
- **Secondary audience:** Early-stage founders, freelancers and small business owners who want to automate repetitive work, manage enquiries and build more efficient systems.
- **Pain points:** Learning tools without earning; no clients; overwhelmed by options; low-paid local jobs; no system
- **Goals:** First online income, dollar earnings, a skill that compounds, own business
- **Knowledge level:** Beginner to intermediate; knows ChatGPT, not workflows
- **Audience stages:** Stuck · Skilled · Paid · Scaled

## 4. Content pillars (topic weightage)

| Pillar | Weight | What it covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI to Income | 45% | Skills, offers and workflows that turn AI into money |
| AI Leverage | 25% | Doing more with less: automation, systems, productivity |
| Proof / Journey | 20% | Your own results, experiments, behind the scenes |
| Psychology of Execution | 10% | Discipline, consistency, decision making |

## 5. Content jobs

| Job | Weight | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reach | 50% | New viewers: broad pain, strong hook, shareable |
| Trust | 35% | Show depth: frameworks, proof, how-tos |
| Conversion | 15% | Move people to DM / offer |

## 6. Growth strategy (scaling and expansion)

- **Current phase:** 90-Day Launch / Anchor
- **Allowed topic space now:** Only the 4 pillars above. No news commentary unless tied to income.
- **Phase rules:** Prioritise Reach + Trust; max 1 Conversion video per week.
- **Expand when:** After the 90-day anchor phase, with 10k followers OR 3 videos over 100k views and clear business-owner interest → add pillar “AI for Business Owners” at 15%
- **Next phase:** Authority / Client Acquisition — introduce AI for Business Owners (15%); rebalance AI to Income to 40%, AI Leverage to 20%, Proof / Journey to 15% and Psychology of Execution to 10%; prioritise real case studies, practical breakdowns and specific DM offers while keeping Reach + Trust the majority.

## 7. Formats and cadence

- **Videos per week:** 5 (Mon–Fri)
- **Stories:** 2–3 per day
- **Carousels:** 1–2 per week, made from that week’s best final script
- **Default length:** 25–35 seconds; 40–50 s only for genuinely deep educational topics

## 10. Guardrails

- **Topics to avoid:** Generic AI news without a skills / income angle; random tool roundups; unrelated politics or celebrity commentary; get-rich-quick promises; empty motivation; fake client wins; unverified earning claims; unrelated trends and clickbait.
- **Claims that need evidence:** income figures, client results, time saved, market stats, anything about “2026 trends”
- **Never fabricate:** income, ROI, client results, quotes or personal experiences. If a script needs my proof and I haven’t given it, stop and mark **CREATOR INPUT REQUIRED**.
- **Originality:** Take topics, pains and hook styles from other creators, never their wording or unique examples.

## 12. Script templates

**Educational Short (default):** Hook (5 options) → Setup / Tension (4) → Problem Deepening (4) → Reframe (4) → Core Value (4) → Example (4) → Payoff (4, 2+ quotable) → CTA (4)

**Storytelling:** Hook → Context → Tension → Turning Point → Lesson → Payoff → CTA

**List:** Hook → Criteria → Item 1 → Item 2 → Item 3 → Recommendation → CTA
